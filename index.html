🏐 Safety first, athlete!

  • Warm up 5–8 minutes (easy jog, arm circles, bodyweight squats) and cool down after.
  • Land soft and quiet: hips and knees bend, and the whole foot settles. Stiff toe-only pops are only for tiny pogo hops.
  • Quality over quantity. Hard sets stay short (about 3–6) with full rest. Stop while reps still look smooth.
  • Jump 2–3 days a week, not back-to-back, and not the day before a match.
  • Warm-Up #1 and #2 are lighter sessions. They do not replace the warm-up above before a Lite, Moderate, or Advanced day.
  • Barbells, Olympic lifts, depth jumps, and hurdle jumps are in Advanced. A qualified coach stays with you for those.
  • Use a low, stable box or step. Do not stack furniture. Do not max out a lift.
  • Stop for sharp pain (that is different from muscle effort) and tell a parent or coach. Nothing here promises fewer injuries or extra inches.
